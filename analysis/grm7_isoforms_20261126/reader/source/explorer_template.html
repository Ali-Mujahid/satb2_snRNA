Five annotated transcripts do not imply five reconstructed or functional isoforms. This 10x nuclear RNA analysis distinguishes diagnostic feature support, conservative splice-supported assignments and overlapping transcript-compatible upper bounds. Transcript annotations and strength of support

Grm7-201 and Grm7-202 encode the complete B-like and A-like receptor models, respectively. Grm7-203 and Grm7-205 have incomplete coding starts; Grm7-204 is annotated as nonsense-mediated decay. Grm7-205 shares a B-like tail but does not establish a third complete receptor.

Replicated support is a descriptive flag: at least three conservative splice-supported UMIs in at least two mice. The threshold is not a hypothesis test. Diagnostic exon-only signal can arise from unprocessed RNA and does not reconstruct a complete transcript.
