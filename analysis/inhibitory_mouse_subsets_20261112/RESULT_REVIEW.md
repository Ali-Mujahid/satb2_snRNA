# Seurat inhibitory mouse-subset sensitivity — results and interpretation

The user requested a search for mouse-inclusion combinations with more DESeq2 calls. This is a sample-subset sensitivity analysis of the real data, not a null simulation, power simulation, or independent replication. Six WT were retained while all 42 possible cKO subsets of sizes 3–6 were fit; six separate WT leave-one-out comparisons with all six cKO were also planned. 21 populations (20 clusters and total) yielded 1008 planned combinations, 928 fitted and 80 untestable, with no remaining errors. Excluding both WT and cKO together or two or more WT was not evaluated. Original Seurat labels, per-mouse integer pseudobulk counts, frozen gene universe, DESeq2 poscounts/Wald ~condition, alpha .05, per-fit independent filtering and BH were retained. A selected mouse absent from a cluster causes that subset to be NOT_TESTED, without silently substituting another mouse. C18 is untestable because it lacks sufficient mice; C20 has 16 rather than 42 eligible cKO subsets because cKO_991 has no nuclei in that cluster. For C20 historical baseline is the preexisting 6 WT/5 cKO model, not an eligible six-cKO fit. Very sparse populations are exploratory, even when minimum three mice per group is met.

The common-subset comparison uses 18 clusters C1–C17 and C19, all with 42 valid cKO-subset fits. The complete original group yields 53 significant cluster–gene calls. Maximum among those 42 for the same subset across all 18 clusters is 198 cluster–gene calls, net +145: 31 shared with full baseline, 167 newly crossing FDR .05 and 22 original calls no longer crossing (8 have unavailable subset FDR; do not label unavailable as tested nonsignificant). There are 168 distinct stable gene IDs among the 198 compared with 49 distinct in the 53 baseline calls; this is not +145 unique genes. Keep all six WT and cKO_1138, cKO_1139, cKO_991; exclude cKO_1160, cKO_1165, cKO_933. The combined inhibitory total is a separate, overlapping endpoint: 4 DEGs at full baseline versus 60 in this same subset, 25 up and 35 down, 4 retained and 56 additional calls, not to be added to the 198. C1 27→112 (15 retained, 97 newly crossing, 12 no longer crossing); C2 0→26; C6 22→18; C16 0→22 with minimum 4 nuclei in a mouse. Clusterwise maximum results can use different mouse subsets and must not be summed.

Other cluster-specific maxima: C2 0→30 and C6 22→23 with 6 WT and cKO_1138, cKO_1139, cKO_1160, cKO_991 (exclude 1165/933). C7 max 3 in two tied subsets. The full range and all ties, exact membership and minimum nuclei are saved in reviewed_results. WT one-at-a-time comparisons are separate: C1 27→34 excluding WT_1123; C6 22→24 excluding WT_1123 or WT_1220; total 4→8 excluding WT_1226. These are again exploratory, not recommended exclusions.

Prior sample metadata record cKO_1138 and cKO_991 in the second sequencing group and cKO_1139 in the main group with all WT. Thus the selected maximum subset is strongly imbalanced by recorded group and genotype, and contains only one cKO mouse in the WT group; sequencing manifests remain unverified. No lane or batch adjustment was fitted in this subset search, and a sequencing effect cannot be separated reliably from biology here. Neither the number 198 nor the selected FDR values account for looking across many possible subsets. Selecting mice because this improves DEG counts is outcome-driven and can bias estimates and apparent significance. Retain full analysis and independently motivated QC exclusions, not the maximum, for confirmatory claims. Three cKO mice have weak uncertainty estimation; pseudo-bulk total additionally mixes inhibitory subtypes.

Independent reconciliation: all 1008 scenario IDs are present once; 469 previously fitted and 459 zero-hit reporting errors recovered as separately executed successful fits; 80 remain NOT_TESTED and zero ERROR; significant-hit records match per-scenario counts; n_up+n_down equals n_deg, and each input count matrix SHA256 recorded. Original independent FDR <.05 applies separately per population and subset; it does not control error over the selection search. Gene-call frequency across the 42 eligible cKO sets counts all fitted sets in the denominator, including models for which a given gene's adjusted p-value was NA; it is descriptive stability, not a confidence probability. RNA estimates do not establish protein/synaptic/physiological function.
