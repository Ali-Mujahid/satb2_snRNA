# Prespecified sensitivity search over mouse subsets

Question: how much can Seurat inhibitory cluster WT–cKO DESeq2 results change when subsets of biological mice are omitted? This is exhaustive within the stated subset family, not a permutation, power simulation, or exhaustive search of all 12-mouse subsets. Each cluster and the inhibitory total are distinct endpoints. For each endpoint test all 42 combinations of 3, 4, 5 or 6 cKO mice retaining all 6 WT mice, plus 6 leave-one-WT-out comparisons retaining all 6 cKO mice, i.e. 48 planned membership sets. Each mouse must have at least one nucleus in that cluster; if any requested mouse is absent or fewer than 3 eligible mice per condition, report NOT_TESTED rather than silently changing requested membership. Hence no C18 inference is forced. Do not fit cross-combinations that remove both genotypes or exclude 2+ WT; these are outside this planned family. WT/cKO exchange or clustering changes are not performed.

Inputs: frozen original-full Seurat inhibitory population integer UMI pseudobulks, 20 clusters plus total; 12 biological mice; frozen original gene prefilter and annotation mapping; remove genes with zero total after restriction. Existing original historical DESeq2 fits provide baseline and numerical validation. DESeq2 ~condition, cKO/WT Wald, poscounts size factors, parametric fit, no automatic outlier replacement, native independent filtering and BH per endpoint/subset at alpha .05. Keep actual package versions and warnings, source hash manifest and exact included/excluded mice, status, counts and all significant-gene records; each fit uses its own normalization/independent filtering. No small-cluster cutoff other than >=1 nucleus per selected mouse; low coverage flagged, not treated as proof. Each model uses genuine mice, no edgeR, no pseudoreplication, no simulated artificial data, no sample imputation. Use four cores (one per endpoint) and deterministic scenario enumeration. All outputs in new directory; preserve previous results.

Interpretation gates: maximum DEG number is a selected maximum from a dependent search and has no generalization guarantee. BH controls its individual fit family under assumptions, not over this subset search. Do not adopt a best-performing exclusion or falsely describe uncorrected post-selection significance as discovery; such comparisons diagnose fragility and motivate independent validation. Report baseline, full range/distribution, all ties, number of attempted/not-tested/error models, gained/lost and identities, stability, included/excluded sample IDs, WT/cKO replicate count and sequencing groups; keep missing FDR distinct. Compare some high-count options to known same partition but do not call unverified 933 another lane. Candidate follow-up not proof of biology.
