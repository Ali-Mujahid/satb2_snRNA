## Interpretation limits

Motif-supported regulons are predicted gene sets; motif annotation and positive coexpression do not prove direct binding or transcriptional activation in these nuclei. Native AUCell is target-expression enrichment and is not source-TF RNA or a genotype fold change. Gene sets, backgrounds and absolute scores differ between the CA1 and inhibitory fits. Source counts and target coverage require interpretation alongside any inferred activity. Genotype partly confounds technical partition; smaller sensitivity cohorts have reduced power. Original Satb2 gene RNA was higher in cKO CA1, so RNA scores alone do not validate functional deletion or protein loss. Installed/controller closure and stochastic native implementations impose documented reproducibility limits despite recorded versions, seeds and hashes.
