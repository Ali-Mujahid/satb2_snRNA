All-pair DE overlaps keyed by stable IDs, not ambiguous gene symbols.

The chosen cohort was discovered by maximizing calls over many dependent subsets. Per-model BH FDR does not adjust for selecting the subset. Recorded sequencing groups are unbalanced by genotype (two selected cKO in another group, one in the WT group) and are unverified against original sequencing manifests; no lane-adjusted or causal interpretation is justified. C16 includes an individual mouse with only 4 nuclei and other rare clusters also have low coverage; such results are exploratory. The 198 calls represent 168 unique genes and cannot be interpreted as independent validation or a reason to remove mice. Original full cohort remains the appropriate reference absent independent prespecified exclusion criteria.
