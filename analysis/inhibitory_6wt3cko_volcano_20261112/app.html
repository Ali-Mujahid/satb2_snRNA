Exploratory selected cohort: these mice were selected after comparing many subsets. The reported within-model FDR does not adjust for that search. The cKO samples are imbalanced across recorded sequencing groups; those group labels remain unverified. Do not treat these results as confirmatory or use the DEG count alone to exclude animals.

Cluster-level results and nuclei

WT and cKO percentages are their shares of captured nuclei in this selected cohort, not biological abundance estimates. Shared-up and shared-down require significance in both fits and the same effect direction. The all-inhibitory total overlaps the clusters and is not added to their counts. C18 and C20 were not testable for this exact selection; the historical full C20 fit used six WT and five cKO mice because one mouse had no C20 nuclei.
