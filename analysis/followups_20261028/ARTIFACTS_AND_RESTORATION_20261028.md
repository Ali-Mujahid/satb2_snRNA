# SATB2 follow-up files and restoration

These additive results address four separately requested questions. The archives preserve native table, model, figure and source bytes. Previous Scanpy/Seurat analyses, original DESeq2 fits, strict DEG-only communication records and regulatory results remain separate and unchanged. Completion of a workflow is distinct from a valid biological conclusion.

## Biological units and interpretation

Mice are the independent biological replicates: six WT and six cKO in the full cohort, with six WT and three cKO in the shared sequencing partition. Nuclei, tools, sampler repetitions and multiple communication methods are not independent biological replication. Full-cohort genotype is partially confounded with sequencing partition. Model scopes and inference families remain separate, and overlapping nucleus subsets must not be added together.

Composition measures relative recovered-nucleus proportions, not absolute tissue abundance or cell loss. Saved mixed/uncertain target and reference identities remain uncertain. The excitatory references retain immune/glial-associated signature-review flags. Numerical convergence cannot resolve them. CA1 deep/superficial labels describe molecular expression programs, not independently localized physical layers. Native scCODA posterior inclusion probabilities and expected Bayesian FDR differ from gene-level DESeq2 statistics.

CellChat retains the requested original-DESeq2 DEG restriction at both ligand and receptor ends, including required complex subunits and their original direction of change. Full DEG tables are retained for provenance; their presence does not authorize non-DEG interactions. Signaling scores infer expression-supported possibilities; they are not measured protein binding or synaptic contact. Mouse-level condition tests remain distinct from cell-label permutation scores and original gene-level DESeq2 inference.

Monocle3 graph-associated genes and modules describe variation along expression-state graphs. They are not new DESeq2 genotype fits or proof of a temporal progression. Arbitrary root checks remain clearly labeled. Existing DESeq2 tables remain the evidence for gene–genotype associations.

RNA velocity remains INPUT_BLOCKED: genuine matched spliced/unspliced counts are unavailable, LiSC access is unresolved and one sample's BAM availability is uncertain. No vectors or latent times were fabricated, no mouse was silently dropped and total counts were not substituted for splicing layers.

## Opening and restoring

The reader, PNG images, PDF figures and text tables can be used without rerunning analysis. Keep native directories together: Monocle3 nearest-neighbor indexes support saved CDS objects, and native chain arrays belong with their tables and diagnostics. TSV/CSV/TXT files are text; MTX.GZ sparse integer counts require their companion genes/cells files; NPZ files preserve native arrays; H5AD files use AnnData; RDS files require compatible R and recorded packages. Original gene-symbol/mapping files and native schemas remain unchanged.

Each of the six component ZIPs contains ARCHIVE_MANIFEST.json with canonical original sources, member sizes and SHA256 values. The outer delivery receipt supplies each whole-ZIP byte size and SHA256. The additional flat offline ZIP instead contains exactly the complete reader files and READER_MANIFEST.json, without archival wrapper additions. For full shared-input alias restoration, use the provided standard-library extract_reviewed_archive.py on a compatible Python (3.10 or later), the exact archive path, a fresh destination and --expected-sha256 with the actual full ZIP digest. It verifies safe members and exact bytes, restores requested aliases and never executes archived code. Ordinary ZIP tools work for non-deduplicated native components; the shared archive requires alias restoration to recreate every requested path.

Archived configurations retain original Mac/server paths for provenance. To rerun on another system, make configuration copies with adjusted extracted paths and use the supplied exact locks, package/source receipts and external reference requirements. Mutable installed environments and caches are deliberately excluded; an archived Mac runtime is not a universal installer. No installation or rerun is performed by extraction. New scientific execution is separate from restoring the results.

Presentation colors, plot sizes, label formatting, selections and PDF downloads operate on saved data. Such display controls do not refit native models, revise eligibility, change biological units or resolve uncertain labels. Original native figures and corrected display-only figures are both retained where applicable.
