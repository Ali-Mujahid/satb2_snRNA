# Completed DESeq2 revision: effect and support review

The native 12-mouse revision is complete. Independent Python/R validation checked 556,722,540 integer entries in 1,437 prepared/final population arrays with zero differences. The three DESeq2 producers published 1,060 endpoints: 964 tested and 96 unsupported. They used 360 fresh fits and 604 exact unchanged or repeated endpoint reuses. Recorded model warnings and errors are zero. This review reads produced results and audits only; it performs no fitting or raw-nucleus aggregation.

The shared totals have exactly the same counts and six numerical result fields across Scanpy and Seurat when the produced decimal values are parsed with Float64 round-trip precision. These totals use one DESeq2 implementation, so their equality demonstrates count and implementation reproducibility rather than independent biological replication. Tool-specific clusters and reviewed disjoint cluster unions remain different population definitions.

The native core flag requires BH<0.05 and the same nonzero direction in all three PRIMARY count branches, finite/tested same-direction estimates in both original partition sensitivities, and all 12 finite/tested same-direction leave-one-mouse-out estimates. It does not require BH significance in every sensitivity refit. Partition and leave-one-out engines are DESeq2 only. Known-partition and main-partition contrasts are supported mainly by the shared main partition's six WT and three cKO mice; their agreement is not independent replication. The original full cohort and paired cohort differ, while paired raw versus corrected results hold nuclei, labels and gene universe fixed.

Every requested PRIMARY endpoint has a source-bound export/coverage audit: 948 endpoints, with 852 tested and 96 unsupported. IN includes every nonempty positive-library mouse aggregate, minimum one nucleus, without downsampling. It requires at least three independent mice in each genotype, at least two residual degrees of freedom, and gene counts of at least five in at least three eligible mice. Seventeen tested IN PRIMARY endpoints per branch include a mouse contribution below five nuclei and remain explicitly exploratory; the eighteenth low-coverage flag per branch belongs to an unsupported endpoint. Label confidence remains a separate question. Other populations require at least 20 nuclei per included mouse. Of tested PRIMARY endpoints, 844 record an actual parametric dispersion fit and eight record local fits following the parametric request; these adaptations are retained in the audit.

Sensitivity-supported illustrative effects, selected by original PRIMARY adjusted P value without a fold-change cutoff, include Adamts3 and Sema3a increases in All/EX; Col5a2 decrease and Sema3a increase in CA1/ProS; and Apool, Arhgap31 decreases with Pdia5 increase in IN. The three IN flags bind stable IDs ENSMUSG00000025525, ENSMUSG00000022799 and ENSMUSG00000022844 respectively. Original IN log2 fold changes are −1.318, −0.815 and +1.064; corrected estimates are −1.595, −0.943 and +1.056. Their existence does not establish equivalence of every fine inhibitory subtype or absence of other inhibitory responses.

Satb2 binds ENSMUSG00000038331. Relative original gene-body RNA is higher in cKO for All (+0.971), EX (+0.956) and CA1/ProS (+1.166), each meeting the core flag. IN Satb2 is not significant (original log2FC −0.072, adjusted P approximately one); the larger corrected estimate is likewise not significant. The prior study's validated deletion/protein context is retained. Gene-level 3′/gene-body counts do not establish exon-4 retention, protein expression or knockout effectiveness, and this review does not reopen genotype validation.

CA1-associated marker behavior is heterogeneous: Ndst4 +0.788, Col11a1 +0.504, Ak5 +0.334 and Calb1 +1.506; Asic2 −0.364 and Pcdh7 −0.326. These six meet the core flag and remain same-direction under correction and all 12 omissions. Thsd7b is approximately unchanged (+0.006; adjusted P0.98); Dkk3 −0.175 has adjusted P0.19. This supports changes in associated expression programs, not a uniform anatomical layer reassignment or physical layer loss. Same-name deep program overlap satisfies the declared reciprocal guard, whereas same-name superficial overlap fails on one side (44.9%). Different-label/Hungarian program comparisons are population-definition sensitivity rather than equivalent superficial populations.

BH correction is within each endpoint, not across all overlapping cluster/category hypotheses. Missing P values/FDR and seven corrected IN genes outside the realized endpoint matrix remain unavailable rather than nonsignificant. CellBender is a qualified count sensitivity; strong effect agreement does not imply identical discovery lists or establish the physical source of removed RNA.
