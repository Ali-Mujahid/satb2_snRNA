Takeaway: The 12-mouse SATB2 reanalysis supports consistent coarse results but sensitive fine CA1 partitions.

This reanalysis asks whether the twelve-mouse SATB2 single-nucleus study gives consistent results across Scanpy and Seurat while preserving independent mice for genotype comparisons. The native workflow completed all twenty-five representation datasets, three mouse-level pseudobulk branches and the final tool comparison, with exact agreement between independent R and Python count aggregation. Integration sensitivity, uncertain fine identities and incomplete convergence evidence prevent blanket biological acceptance. The completed count-sensitivity and Cck reviews support prioritizing mouse-supported core effects while retaining provisional fine identities.

## Scientific context and question

The study contains six independent WT and six Satb2 cKO P60 male mice, with one GEM-X 3-prime v4 capture per mouse and whole CA1, including ventral tissue, documented in the prior study records. CA1 neuronal and interneuron expression are the biological interests. The supplied inputs are existing Cell Ranger gene-by-nucleus matrices and accepted earlier QC/annotation records. This is a post-count reanalysis; it does not independently recount FASTQs or assess chemistry, alignment or exon-specific deletion. WT and cKO mice are unpaired biological replicates; paired count branches refer to the same nuclei measured with different count sources. Mice are the experimental units. Nuclei describe populations and within-population expression; they are not independent genotype replicates.

The comparison intentionally fixes the accepted QC roster, original-query atlas labels and population-specific feature panels. It tests implementation and representation sensitivity under a shared design, rather than independently applying each package's default QC and feature-selection policies. MapMyCells supplies common reference annotation evidence, not a third independent clustering or differential-expression engine. Cumulus/Cell Ranger and SCSEQ were not executed in this benchmark.

## Interpretation

Coarse molecular organization and estimated core genotype effects are reasonably consistent under the controlled benchmark. Fine EX/CA1-ProS partitions depend more strongly on implementation, integration and count source. Sample Harmony can remove genotype-associated structure because sample identity is nested within genotype. Original Seurat CA1-ProS condition-versus-cluster NMI changes from 0.23176 to 0.00599 with Harmony, documenting sensitivity rather than establishing its cause. R original-global log, paired-original-global log and SCT reach the ten-iteration cap without reported convergence. Python actual iteration counts and convergence histories are `NOT_RECORDED`; finite coordinates establish neither convergence nor cap exhaustion.

Existing marker evidence supports major inhibitory Sst/Pvalb/Vip programs while retaining mixed or weak rare groups. CA1-ProS contains coherent Ndst4/Col11a1/Epha10 deep-associated and Thsd7b/Pcdh7/Lingo2 superficial-associated workbook programs, alongside unspecified/mixed programs. They are expression programs, not physical layers, and this broad cohort includes ProS. The original Scanpy 93-nucleus CA1-ProS mixed-signature group has immune-associated leading markers despite frozen singlet calls and broad high-support atlas predictions. Seurat separately identifies mixed candidates with 31 IN nuclei and 99 CA1-ProS nuclei; neither has a mouse with twenty nuclei. One 970-nucleus Seurat CA1-ProS group has weak detection-specificity differences despite many rank-test marker rows. The full-gene [marker-output review](/Users/mujahid357/Documents/codex_new/SATB2_scanpy_seurat_mapmycells_20261013/reviews/seurat_completed_marker_output_review_v1) preserves these qualifications. The 93-nucleus Scanpy and 31/99-nucleus Seurat rare groups have inadequate per-mouse coverage for genotype inference. The separate 970-nucleus group meets original mouse-coverage requirements but has weak marker specificity. No new exclusion or hard identity is justified by these marker observations. [Identity evidence](/Users/mujahid357/Documents/codex_new/SATB2_scanpy_seurat_mapmycells_20261013/reviews/CA1_ProS_program_identity_support.tsv) and [carried QC evidence](/Users/mujahid357/Documents/codex_new/SATB2_scanpy_seurat_mapmycells_20261013/reviews/primary_CA1_ProS_cluster8_existing_QC_reference.tsv) preserve these qualifications.

## Limitations and blockers

The native workflow is complete with no current execution blocker. The detailed native partition PDF has a minor xlabel/footer collision; the reviewed compact heatmaps are preferred for presentation. The requested outputs and supported comparisons are complete; remaining uncertainty concerns biological interpretation and fine identity. Common QC/features/labels make the benchmark controlled but limit independence. Workbook panels are context-specific; 145 of 147 rows map exactly, while AC129186.1 is unavailable in this feature reference. Atlas agreement and UMAP proximity cannot establish anatomy, biological correctness or preservation of genotype effects. Marker tests use sampled nuclei for descriptive cluster identity; Seurat marker adjustment is full-gene Bonferroni, distinct from replicate-level pseudobulk BH. Broad totals and overlapping exploratory endpoints do not establish a physical layer shift, cell loss or causal mechanism. Technical integrity and supported computational comparisons are accepted for this benchmark; causal mechanisms, physical layers and rare-cell identities are not established. Exact upstream GTF/intron-count settings were not independently recovered, and no FASTQ realignment was performed.
