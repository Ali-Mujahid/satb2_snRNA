What the evidence shows. Gene effects are saved mouse-level DESeq2 cKO versus WT results. The ligand–receptor panel contains 16 pooled population-specific records where both gene components were significant in their own comparisons. These are hypotheses, not measurements of protein binding, synapses or inhibitory current. Smaller LIANA magnitude rank means higher relative rank in that original scoring scope; it is not a genotype P value.

Plot appearance

1 · Candidate gene effects

Each dot is a gene's cKO/WT log2 fold change in the selected saved population. Filled colored dots pass adjusted P <0.05; outlined gray dots were tested but not significant. A dash means an unavailable adjusted P or effect. This curated panel is not a complete gene search.

2 · RNA evidence across individual mice

Each dot is one mouse's mean per-nucleus log1p(CP10k), using the frozen population and original expression data. These are descriptive means, not DESeq2 pseudobulk coefficients or independent nuclei-level tests.

3 · Strict ligand–receptor candidates

Dot position is the native LIANA magnitude rank in the pooled 5-endpoint scope (smaller ranks are higher). Dot area reflects the smaller detected fraction across partner subunits, with a minimum display size. Color indicates the ligand’s cKO/WT gene direction; receptor directions are in the table/download. Source → target is the resource orientation, not measured excitation or inhibition.

Priorities: inhibitory Sst-reference C6 Cntn3 ↓ with CA1 Ptprg ↑ is a contact hypothesis, informed by published CNTN3–PTPRG binding evidence; CA1 Ptn ↓ with Pvalb-reference C1 Ptprz1 ↑ is a hypothesis informed by independent adult CA1 Satb2/PTN experiments. Ptn–Ptprz1 C1 or superficial specificity is not established. The Nrxn1/Nlgn1 pair also has weak cell-label specificity.

4 · Network context

In the pooled CA1 hdWGCNA network, Ptn belongs to the purple coexpression module (within-module rank 7; kME 0.491). The oriented module eigengene is lower in cKO: deep-associated effect −2.073 (FDR 7.10×10⁻⁶), superficial-associated −2.356 (FDR 3.54×10⁻⁸); both retained significance in the separately adjusted shared-partition sensitivity. These are continuous module-score differences, not gene log2 fold changes. The gene and module analyses reuse the same data; coexpression does not establish direct SATB2 binding or pathway activation.

Motif-supported direct regulation and a SATB2 regulon are not established by this view. Open the reviewed regulatory atlas.

Interpretation for electrophysiology

Deep-associated = C1+C5; superficial-associated = C2+C3+C8. These are molecular programs, not validated anatomical locations. IN C1 is Pvalb-reference supported; IN C6 is Sst-reference supported; IN C8 has mixed reference subclasses. Prioritize spatial and protein localization, then compare identified PV+ and SST+ inputs to superficial and deep CA1 pyramidal neurons across independent mice. Gene-level effects do not determine the direction of a current or establish that cKO superficial neurons have become deep neurons.
