# SCENE CA1 explorer: provenance

Completed local analysis: 600 epochs, ZIP likelihood, 16 latent dimensions, seed 42, 2,518 most-detected and selected marker genes, 27,703 CA1/ProS nuclei and 12 mouse batch categories. Source data are the original-full Seurat frozen annotations and the original raw UMI count matrix; SCENE is a Python model fitted to those Seurat-defined nuclei, not a Seurat package function or an anatomical map. Selected gene positions in the joint 2D projection cluster away from the nuclei and can optionally be displayed one at a time. They should not be used to infer biological proximity from UMAP.

Cluster marker colors are gene-wise z-scores of pooled cluster mean log1p(CP10k) (computed across all existing cluster rows); layer colors are pooled nucleus-weighted mean log1p(CP10k). Dot area is proportional to the measured fraction of nuclei with a nonzero count. Grouped 30-marker selection is descriptive, not a differential-expression test; chosen genes are not necessarily unique to the named cluster. Deep-associated is C1+C5 and superficial-associated is C2+C3+C8. Mixed and uncertain clusters are not relabeled. Cluster visibility controls do not recalculate the fixed layer aggregates; the genotype filter applies to the UMAP only, and marker summaries remain pooled. No DE or statistical test is computed in the page. A mouse adjustment can remove genotype-related variation; spatial arrangements and shape are not histological locations.

Downloads reflect the current plotted views. SVG downloads contain an embedded raster image, not individually editable vector dots. The MP4 is a 15-second slideshow with cross-fades between the original SCENE projection and marker dotplot; it is not RNA velocity, a cell trajectory, or a dynamic model.

Interactive UMAP coordinates are rounded to 0.01 UMAP units for compact delivery; saved source coordinates and analysis are unchanged.

The marker selector shows 58 saved gene coordinates from the same SCENE joint UMAP. The gene star is a projected gene location, not expression in an individual nucleus; the map may expand to include it. UMAP distances can distort the underlying 16-dimensional geometry.

Dynamic video: 28-second orthographic rotating camera around a 3D PCA projection jointly fitted to the final 16D cell and gene coordinates; 29.0% variance retained. Points retain their fitted positions and no intermediate training epochs, inferred trajectories, anatomical positions or biological movement are simulated. The opening 11-by-30 heatmap and marker order are derived from the saved Seurat marker summaries. Original static slideshow is retained separately.

The 40-second v3 video shows the cluster pheatmap in two readable 15-gene panels, followed by 18 nucleus–gene expression edges and rotating cluster and layer views with six actual gene positions. Edges are three nuclei per gene chosen from measured nonzero raw UMI counts by proximity in the full 16D embedding; they illustrate detection, not ligand–receptor interactions or connectivity. Training-time positions were not saved and are not reconstructed. The displayed 3D coordinates are PCA of the fixed final 16D fit and retain 29% of variance. See the accompanying video source, receipt and expression-edge audit under analysis/scene_20261111.

The clear layer-by-layer v4 video begins with 96 real nuclei × 16 genes (four randomly chosen nuclei per mouse per layer group; seed 42). Teal encodes log1p raw UMI counts, clipped above 10 for display only. It then shows superficial-associated and deep-associated cells separately, without refitting the embedding, on the same rotating 3D PCA coordinates and scale. Twelve cyan edges in each view join gene positions to sampled nuclei with actual nonzero counts; they are expression evidence, not cell–cell interactions. Heatmap source counts, mouse/condition metadata and a count-edge audit excluding nucleus barcode IDs are in analysis/scene_20261111. The 3D PCA retains 29% of final 16D variance; camera motion is not a developmental or model-training trajectory.
