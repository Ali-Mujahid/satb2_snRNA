## Interpretation limits

The strict both-end-DEG selection reuses the same study before interaction-score testing. Score P/BH values are exploratory conditional on that selection and do not establish independently validated differential communication. Genotype partly overlaps technical partition; unrestricted full-cohort exchangeability and causal genotype interpretation are limited. Shared-partition analysis has fewer cKO mice and its exact P resolution is1/84. No gene DE model was refitted, and native cell-label P is never genotype P or FDR.

The database subset cannot describe complete hippocampal communication. C8 has no qualifying strict DEG edge, not proven absence of communication. CellChat and previous LIANA databases differ, so pair-list coverage differences are not biological contradictions. All reported partners are candidate molecular interaction machinery. RNA scores do not establish protein abundance, binding, direction of synaptic transmission, pathway activation, anatomical deep/superficial position or functional E/I balance. C8 identity uncertainty remains unchanged.

4. Circle graphs are readable with fixed endpoint colors/common edge-width scale; the native circle function does not visibly retain the requested header. A separate explicit in-page caption can identify method/direction/condition without changing values.

Preserve all sealed originals. Save an explicit native-to-corrected figure mapping, file hashes and display-only validation. No new inference/permutation/eligibility/statistics is justified by these display defects.
