Findings and downloads

In all CA1/ProS candidate nuclei, exon-support pairs were 0–3 per cKO mouse and 21–29 per WT mouse. The exon-skipping junction had 21–46 pairs in each cKO and zero observed under the filters in each WT. The C11-excluded and cKO_933-excluded sensitivities retain the qualitative pattern. These findings support genotype-associated exon skipping; RNA evidence does not establish complete knockout or prove genomic or protein loss.
