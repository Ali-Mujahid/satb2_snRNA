Method and scope

Full tested gene-level cKO-versus-WT DESeq2 results are mapped onto mouse ligand–receptor resource pairs. Detection is calculated separately in WT and cKO; all required subunits must meet the threshold in the same condition. WT and cKO expression summaries reuse the same DE fit. A 10% threshold is a sensitivity view. A jointly eligible mouse has at least five nuclei in both paired populations; support means all required subunits pass the threshold in that mouse. Majority support means more than half of jointly eligible mice in at least one genotype support the pair. A mathematical majority can occur with only one eligible mouse; the separate minimum-three-eligible-mice option is a more informative descriptive prioritization. Neither flag is a statistical test of altered interaction strength.

Each subunit retains its own gene-level result. Multiple significant CA1 subunits can change in opposite directions; missing IN results are not zero and nonsignificant results do not establish no effect. Results selected using CA1 DE on the same dataset are exploratory; transcript presence does not establish protein abundance, local contact, or altered signalling. Technical and biological confounders and limited mice affect inference. The original strict both-end-DEG analysis remains separately available in the communication atlas. The frozen interaction resource does not label the signalling mechanism.
