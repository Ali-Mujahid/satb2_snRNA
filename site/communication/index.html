What these results support

The pooled analysis prioritizes 324 replicated, context-specific, DEG-supported population × LR records: 112 CA1→inhibitory and 212 inhibitory→CA1. Of these, 322 have CA1-only matching gene-level DEG evidence and two have evidence at both ends. This is an exploratory shortlist, not a new genotype FDR test.

IN C6 Cntn3 → CA1 Ptprg is the clearest both-end lead. Expression support is present in all six WT and all six cKO mice. Cntn3 decreases in the Sst-reference-supported C6 population, while Ptprg increases in both CA1 programs. Opposite component RNA changes do not determine a net change in communication.

Why inhibitory evidence is sparse

The four inhibitory-total DEGs have no overlap with this LR resource. Only inhibitory clusters C1 and C6 have resource-overlapping DEGs. Thus many DEG-supported candidates derive their evidence from the CA1 endpoint. C18’s original DESeq2 fit was not tested; it is never represented as zero DEGs.

How to read the numbers

  • magnitude_rank: consensus priority; lower is stronger. It is not a genotype adjusted P value.
  • lr_logfc: native source/target group-versus-rest specificity, not cKO/WT log2 fold change.
  • lr_gmeans: geometric mean of the two endpoint means on the supplied log-normalized expression scale.
  • CellPhoneDB P: within-dataset cell-label specificity from 1,000 permutations; a saved zero is below empirical resolution, not exact zero probability.
  • DEG support: existing DESeq2 adjusted P < 0.05 for the ligand in its source or receptor in its target, excluding ambiguous-symbol primary evidence.
  • Mouse support: counts with the native expression gates passed; eligible denominators are retained. Unavailable groups are not expression zeros.

Evidence tiers

Matching endpoint DESeq2 support requires magnitude rank within your cutoff plus at least one matching endpoint DEG. Replicated + context-specific additionally requires magnitude and specificity ranks ≤0.05, CellPhoneDB P≤0.05, ≥3 supporting mice in each genotype, and a reference-supported atomic inhibitory cluster. Its inhibitory-total view is intentionally empty under that identity policy. All candidates keeps expression-qualified records within your rank cutoff, including identity-review groups.

Frozen inputs and completed methods

29,367 distinct nuclei: 8,750 CA1 deep-program, 11,706 CA1 superficial-program and 8,911 inhibitory nuclei. Six WT and six cKO mice provide independent biological replicates. Total and constituent inhibitory clusters were scored in separate fits. CA1 mixed/weak/conflict programs are outside this scope.

Full-gene integer RNA counts were joined to fixed Seurat memberships. Duplicate symbols were summed before log1p normalization to 10,000 UMIs using the full source library. LIANA 2.0.0 combined its CellPhoneDB, Connectome, logFC, NATMI and SingleCellSignalR methods using frozen mouseconsensus. This is LIANA’s CellPhoneDB scorer, not a separate CellPhoneDB v5 run. Every complex subunit must be detected in ≥10% of a scored group; minimum group size is five nuclei. All six consensus fits and 24 per-mouse support calculations completed.

Limits of the interpretation

Nuclear RNA and a curated prior do not measure extracellular ligand availability, receptor localization, contact, activation, inhibition or functional synapses. Some resource entries need localization review; Rims1→Cacna1c must not be presented as an established secreted-ligand route. Mirrored curated orientations are not independent physiological routes. C8, C12, C16, C18 and C19 retain mixed identity warnings; C20 retains an immune-associated review flag. Fine molecular names do not establish morphology or connectivity.
