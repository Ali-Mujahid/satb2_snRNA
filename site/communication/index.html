Corrected results: ligand AND receptor DEGs

The previous default accepted a DEG at either endpoint. It therefore included non-DEG partners and populations outside your five requested endpoints. This corrected view requires every ligand AND receptor subunit to be a significant, unambiguous DEG in its exact source or target population. Other inhibitory clusters and inhibitory total are excluded.

The pooled dataset contains 16 DEG-only population × ordered LR records: six CA1→inhibitory and ten inhibitory→CA1. WT has 15 and cKO 16. With magnitude rank ≤0.05, these become pooled eight, WT nine and cKO seven. The optional replicated/context-specific shortlist contains two records. These rank thresholds describe native context priority, not genotype significance.

Why C8 has no eligible interaction

C8’s only DEG is Calb1, up in cKO (log2FC +1.93207; adjusted P 0.0154178). Calb1 is absent from mouseconsensus ligand/receptor subunits. C8 remains visible with zero qualifying edges and its mixed-identity review flag. This does not establish absence of biological communication.

How this correction was calculated

The five saved DESeq2 significant-gene lists were checked against their original stable-ID audits. Only matching endpoint symbols with unambiguous identities and adjusted P <0.05 qualify. Every complex subunit must qualify at both ends. The 47 saved rows across pooled/WT/cKO were then selected from completed native LIANA results, preserving every native scalar score.

This is a DEG-only projection of completed LIANA scores. Full-gene normalization and the original population/background scoring context are retained. DESeq2, LIANA and its rank aggregation were not refitted on a truncated DEG matrix. Previous analyses and broad results remain preserved as historical files.

How to read the native scores

  • magnitude_rank: lower is higher consensus priority; not a genotype P value or FDR.
  • lr_logfc: native source/target group-versus-rest specificity, not cKO/WT fold change.
  • lr_gmeans: geometric mean of endpoint means on the native log-normalized expression scale.
  • CellPhoneDB P: cell-label specificity from 1,000 permutations inside a scoring condition; not a differential genotype test. Saved zero is below empirical resolution.
  • Up/down: original gene DESeq2 log2(cKO/WT), with each gene’s adjusted P <0.05. No interaction-activity genotype test was fitted.

Completed methods and limits

Frozen Seurat original_full counts/memberships were used for LIANA 2.0.0 with mouseconsensus and its native CellPhoneDB, Connectome, logFC, NATMI and SingleCellSignalR methods. Original log1p(CP10k) normalization uses the full source library. Every complex subunit must be detected in ≥10% of the scored group; minimum group size five; 1,000 cell-label permutations, seed 1337. Six independent WT and six cKO mice supply gene DESeq2 replication.

Nuclear RNA and curated interaction priors prioritize hypotheses; they do not measure protein availability, localization, contact, activation/inhibition or synapses. Mirrored Nrxn/Nlgn resource orientations are not independent physiological routes. WT/cKO native score or record-count differences are descriptive.
