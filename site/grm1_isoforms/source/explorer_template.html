Grm1: alpha / beta RNA and splice evidence

Twelve P60 mice · six WT and six Satb2 cKO · frozen Seurat populations · superficial CA1 C2+C3 · deep CA1 C1+C5 · original inhibitory clusters

Six annotated RNA models encode two receptor protein forms. Three coding transcripts map to alpha (201) or beta (202/203); three additional models are noncoding. This 10x nuclear RNA analysis distinguishes diagnostic feature support, conservative splice-supported assignments and overlapping transcript-compatible upper bounds. Shared reads are not arbitrarily divided. Zero support does not prove biological absence.

Alpha/beta annotations and strength of support

Grm1-201 → mGluR1 alpha (1199 aa). Grm1-202 and -203 encode identical mGluR1 beta (906 aa) proteins with different UTRs. Grm1-204/-205/-206 are noncoding models. Shared beta reads are counted once at the protein-family level and are not arbitrarily split between transcript IDs.

Conservative alpha/beta counts are uniquely compatible with a protein family, diagnostic and splice-supported; they are evidence lower bounds, not full-length abundance estimates. Replicated support (≥3 UMIs in ≥2 mice) is a descriptive flag, not a hypothesis test.

In Ferraguti et al. 1998, Figure 4B is a hippocampal synaptosome protein blot: alpha ~150 kDa and beta ~97 kDa. “4B” is a panel label, not an isoform or gene name. The blot does not identify the contributing cell types. Grm7 encodes the separate mGlu7A/B receptors.
