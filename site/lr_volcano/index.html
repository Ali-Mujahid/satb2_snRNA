How to read the evidence. Volcano plots show saved gene-level DESeq2 results. By default, a ligand–receptor pair requires every subunit to be significant in its matching sender or receiver population. The optional either-end rule allows an untested or non-DEG partner. Volcano marker text identifies only subunits that are significant in the selected population. The heatmap shows separate gene fold changes, not a measured change in interaction strength. CA1 layer programs are expression-associated, not measured anatomical position. The plots use overlapping populations and independent mice; adjusted P values belong to separate endpoint families.

Ligand–receptor gene-effect heatmap

For each directional pair, tiles show signed cKO/WT gene effects in the exact source or target population. Complex subunits are individually labelled; missing or ambiguous values are gray. Colors do not encode LIANA rank or prove excitation, inhibition, binding, or a change in communication. Ranks are sorted low to high; use the page controls to see all filtered records.

Methods and interpretation

Existing original-full Seurat pseudobulk fits only. Adjusted P values are Benjamini–Hochberg within each fit; missing values remain unavailable. CA1 C8 uses 6 WT and 5 cKO mice. Labeling does not rerun DESeq2 or LIANA. Source→target is database orientation and does not establish physical contact or functional synaptic direction. The same mice and nuclei occur across overlapping populations.

On a static GitHub page the first selection may take a moment to load. Empty pair selections mean no records met the selected criteria, not evidence that cells cannot communicate.
