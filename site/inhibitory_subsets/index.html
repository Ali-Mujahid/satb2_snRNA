All combinations and methods

42 cKO-subset combinations and 6 WT leave-one-out combinations per population were planned: 928 fitted and 80 not tested. C18 was not testable; C20 has fewer eligible subsets. All selected nuclei were used for each mouse; a subset with a requested mouse absent from a cluster was not fitted.
