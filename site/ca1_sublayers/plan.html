## Reassignment rule, to be frozen before seeing newly computed votes

Compare each literature marker in a mixed cluster to WT-only reference profiles, equal-weighted across original anchor clusters: C2/C3/C8 for superficial and C1/C5 for deep. For a superficial-associated marker, a direction-consistent WT anchor difference means mean(superficial) exceeds mean(deep); for deep-associated markers the reverse. A marker votes for whichever anchor its WT cluster mean log1p-CP10k expression is closer to, using the midpoint. Ties cast no vote. Retain individual marker values, mean/detection per mouse, the two anchors, distance to midpoint and vote. Require unique exact mouse symbol, nonzero native WT counts, >=5% detection in at least one anchor, and a >=0.10 unit anchor difference in log1p-CP10k; otherwise that marker is uninformative. Require at least three informative markers from each panel; no evidence or exact 50/50 is unassigned. When superficial votes exceed deep votes, assign the whole source cluster superficial-associated; when deep votes exceed superficial votes, assign deep-associated. Vote percentages are fractions of informative marker votes, not probabilities or percentages of nuclei. Low margins, <70% winner, low replicate/leave-one-marker-out consistency, discordant independent marker evidence, and mixed cluster heterogeneity are flagged and shown, not claimed as established physical layers. Repeat with >=70% as a stricter sensitivity and preserve weak cases rather than forcing them into high-confidence assignment. Freeze the assignments across both genotypes; use WT-only per-mouse sensitivity and inspect changes in cluster composition by mouse/condition. C11 remains separate.

Check heterogeneity at single-nucleus level and, if feasible, run a separately recorded mixed-cluster subdivision sensitivity without using genotype or DE outcomes to tune resolutions. This must not masquerade as anatomical proof, and a cluster-based whole-union comparison remains separate from any nucleus-based alternative. If only sparse expression is available for a nucleus, do not convert lack of detection into true absence.

## Differential expression and comparison

Add each assigned mixed cluster to its existing original program, forming two exact disjoint unions. Original and augmented counts must sum original integer gene-by-mouse atomic pseudobulks; independently verify union memberships and count sums. Use existing DESeq2 original_full settings and seven-column gene-symbol exports: mouse-level raw integer pseudobulk, cKO versus WT, original >=20 nuclei/mouse eligibility, minimum 3 independent mice/group and matching frozen original gene filtering and DESeq2 settings. Retain stable-ID mapping/audit to avoid duplicate symbols or missing symbol conflation. Full cohort primary is exploratory given partial technical/genotype confounding; also run the already-prespecified main-partition 6 WT / 3 cKO sensitivity where eligible, with separate result/FDR families. Do not infer increased significance is evidence of improved biological validity.

Compare each augmented fit with its own original P0244 or P0247 full results by stable ID. Report a full status cross-tab for significant up/down/nonsignificant/unavailable, newly significant up/down, no-longer-significant, sign-flipped, unchanged significant, and genes untestable or outside the other gene universe. Do not call an unavailable result a lost DEG. Report totals, overlap/Jaccard and effect changes, and a shared-tested-gene sensitivity with filtering and FDR definitions made explicit. Adjusted p-values derive from separate fits and changing lists are descriptive; a formal interaction test is a different analysis. Retain counts/coverage, per-mouse UMI totals, size factors, design matrices, fitting warnings, reference/baseline/updated population membership IDs, per-gene status changes and exclusions.
