## Interpretation
The marker-vote fractions are fractions of eligible markers, not probabilities or percentages of superficial/deep nuclei. Low expression of a deep-associated marker can vote toward the superficial reference, even without direct superficial marker enrichment. The C7 result in particular can reflect weak panels rather than a convincing layer identity. All labels are provisional and physical layers remain undetermined; trajectories, direction of cause, and independent validation are not established. Changes in DEG counts may reflect mixed composition, gene filtering and separate multiple-testing universes, not a change in biological validity; shared-gene BH sensitivity is explicitly separate from DESeq2's native adjusted values. Batch/genotype overlap remains partially confounded, and C10 lacks eligible WT mice under the per-mouse marker review rule. Comparison is gene-specific signed cKO/WT Wald tests, not evidence for a significant difference between effect sizes across fits.
