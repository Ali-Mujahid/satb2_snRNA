Controls

Interpretation: All five added clusters have weak marker-vote majorities (53–58%). Votes are fractions of informative marker decisions, not probabilities or proportions of nuclei. The anchors were derived from related marker information, C4 WT mice are split and C10 has no WT mouse meeting the 20-nucleus evidence threshold. CA1/ProS labels indicate expression programs; physical sublayers remain unverified. The full cohort has partial genotype/technical confounding. Changes in gene lists can result from altered population composition or testing universe; they do not establish a difference between two effect sizes.

How the mixed-cluster calls were made

Each of 19 informative markers votes for the closer WT anchor. An absent deep marker can vote towards the superficial anchor; this is not the same as direct expression of a superficial marker. One source marker was not mapped and the replacement Gpc5 was discordant, so it did not vote.

Method, provenance and limitations

The marker list is from the control comparison in the supplied workbook; exact attribution of the workbook to a publication has not been verified. The external study of CA1 laminar organization reports that marker position can vary along the hippocampal axis. Original labels were defined using related markers, so anchor agreement is not independent validation. Satb2 was excluded from scoring. A minimum of three mice per genotype was required for DE; full cohort has six per genotype and the matched-partition sensitivity uses six WT and three cKO. DESeq2 1.53.6 was used for both new baseline and expanded fits; the archived original used 1.52.0. The 70% rule produced no additions. The complete archive records marker lists, votes, exact counts, mouse support, model settings, diagnostics and reproducible source. Read the scientific review and analysis plan.
