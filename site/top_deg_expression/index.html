The initial panel selects up to 10 genes higher and 10 lower in cKO, ranked within each direction by adjusted P. If fewer significant or expression-available genes exist, fewer are shown. Counts in one mouse are one biological replicate; nuclei are not independent replicates. Change genes with the selection controls below.

Population and genes

Only genes with packaged expression can be plotted. Duplicate gene symbols, if present, require an unambiguous ID; missing symbols are reported. Reordering changes display only, not the DESeq2 results.

Colors and size

Mouse-level expression heatmap

Each column is a mouse. Colors are row z-scores of log1p pseudobulk counts per million (CPM) across available mice in this population; gray means unavailable. Values are clipped to the selected z-score limit; a gene with zero variance is shown at zero. This view is ordered as supplied; no clustering dendrogram is implied.

Expression dotplot

Color shows the pooled per-nucleus mean log1p counts per 10,000 (CP10k) for each genotype; dot area represents the fraction of nuclei with detected expression. The two columns summarize WT and cKO, while the heatmap shows the individual mice. Because nuclei are pooled, mice with more nuclei contribute more to these descriptive values; the dotplot is not a differential-expression test.

Interpretation

Selection uses each population’s existing mouse-level DESeq2 results and its own adjusted-P family. A change in gene selection or color does not refit a model. CA1 layer labels refer to expression-associated programs, not confirmed anatomical position. Genes selected as significant in one group are not necessarily significant in another.
